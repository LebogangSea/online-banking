What is online sales

The process of buying and selling goods and services over the internet through digital platforms like websites, mobile apps or online market places.

How online sales work

  1. Digital platforms: Business use various online channels such as E-commerce website, social media pages or online market places to show-case and sell their products and services.
  2. Customer interaction: Customers find products or services online browse their options and add items to a digital shopping cart.
  3. Ordering and payments: Customers can then place orders and make payment securely online using digital devices like computers, tablets or smartphones.
  4. Delivery or Pickup: The products are then deliverd to the customer or made available for pick up often as part of a "click and collect" service.

Aspects/Advantages of online banking

  • Convenience: Customers can stop from the comfort worldwide not just with an internet connection.
  • Global reach: Business can sell to customers worldwide not just in their local area
  • Accessibility: The offer 24/7 availability of online stores meanint that customers can shop anytime.
  • Variety: A wider range of products and services are often available online than in traditional brick-and-motar stores.
  • Direct customer engagement: Online platforms allows businesses to directly interact with customer, gather data and build stronger brand relationships.

Disadvantage of online sales

  1. Security Risk: Concerns about data security and online fraud which can undermine customers trust
  2. Limited Customer Interactions: Lack of face-to-face interactio which can make it difficult to build strong customer relationships.
  3. Reliance on Technology: Dependence on functioning website, internet access and payment systems.
  4. Shipping and logistics: Managing shipping costs and complex fulfilment process at scale can be challenging.
