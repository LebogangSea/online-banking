All about Technology

Technology is the practical application of scientific knowledge to create tools, techniques, and systems that solve problems, achieve goals, and improve human life. It encompasses everything from simple physical objects like hammers and wheels to complex digital systems like computers and the internet, and involves the knowledge needed to design, create, and use these innovations. Technology have contributed to human welfare through increased prosperity, improved comfort and quality of life, and medical progress but they can also disrupt existing social hierarchies, cause pollution, and harm individuals or groups.
