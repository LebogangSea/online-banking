All about Technology

Technology is the practical application of scientific knowledge to create tools, techniques, and systems that solve problems, achieve goals, and improve human life. It encompasses everything from simple physical objects like hammers and wheels to complex digital systems like computers and the internet, and involves the knowledge needed to design, create, and use these innovations. Technology have contributed to human welfare through increased prosperity, improved comfort and quality of life, and medical progress but they can also disrupt existing social hierarchies, cause pollution, and harm individuals or groups.

What is online banking

Online banking is also known as internet banking or vitual banking, is a system that enables customers to conduct a range of financial transactions through a banks website or mobile app.It also allows people to manage their money digitally including checking balances, transferring money or even paying bills without in need to go to the bank physically.

How online banking works

  1. Access: You can access your online banking profile via a web browser on a computer or through your bank’s mobile app on a laptop.
  2. Registrations and Logins: You first register your account with the bank to get a username and password. When you want to bank, you log in using these credentials.
  3. Security: To ensure your accounts is safe banks use multiple security measures-Username and Passwords or Encryption.
  4. Dashboards: After logging in you’ll see a dashboard with your account balances, transaction history, and other features.
  5. Transactions: You can perform various transactions, such as- View account details, Transfer funds, pay bills, manage your cards and applying for loans.

    Disadvantages of Online banking

    1. Hacking and Phishing: Online banking platforms are vulnerable to cyberattacks, phishing scams and identify theft where criminals try to obtain your personal information.
    2. No cash Deposit: Most online banking do not allow you to deposit cash directly you typically need to visit a bank or an ATM that aceepts cash.
    3. Transactions Disputes: Disagreements or errors in online transactions can sometimes require a physical visit to the bank to resolve.

    What is online sales?

    The process of buying and selling goods and services over the internet through digital platforms like websites, mobile apps or online market places.

    How online sales work

    1. Digital platforms: Business use various online channels such as E-commerce website, social media pages or online market places to show-case and sell their products and services.
    2. Customer interaction: Customers find products or services online browse their options and add items to a digital shopping cart.
    3. Ordering and payments: Customers can then place orders and make payment securely online using digital devices like computers, tablets or smartphones.
    4. Delivery or Pickup: The products are then deliverd to the customer or made available for pick up often as part of a "click and collect" service.

    Aspects/Advantages of online banking

    • Convenience: Customers can stop from the comfort worldwide not just with an internet connection.
    • Global reach: Business can sell to customers worldwide not just in their local area
    • Accessibility: The offer 24/7 availability of online stores meanint that customers can shop anytime.
    • Variety: A wider range of products and services are often available online than in traditional brick-and-motar stores.
    • Direct customer engagement: Online platforms allows businesses to directly interact with customer, gather data and build stronger brand relationships.

    Disadvantage of online sales

    1. Security Risk: Concerns about data security and online fraud which can undermine customers trust
    2. Limited Customer Interactions: Lack of face-to-face interactio which can make it difficult to build strong customer relationships.
    3. Reliance on Technology: Dependence on functioning website, internet access and payment systems.
    4. Shipping and logistics: Managing shipping costs and complex fulfilment process at scale can be challenging.
